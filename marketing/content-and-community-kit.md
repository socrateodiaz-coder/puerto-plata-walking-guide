# Puerto Plata Walking Guide — first content and outreach kit

DRAFT / NOT SENT. Brand: Puerto Plata on Foot. Product: self-guided walk for travelers docking at **Taino Bay**. Do not suggest that the route begins at Amber Cove. Promotional copy and purchase links must only be used after final PDF, price, checkout, and facts are approved.

## Brand voice
- Friendly, practical Puerto Plata local; English first for cruise visitors.
- Tell travelers which port you mean, be specific about what you have verified, encourage allowance for ship return and weather, avoid guarantees.
- No fake reviews, fabricated cruise schedules, or unverifiable superlatives.
- Disclose your interest if posting in a group where the business is relevant.

## Owned channels: six initial post concepts

1. Reel: "Taino Bay or Amber Cove? Check your port before planning a walk." Explain these are different ports and this guide starts at Taino Bay.
2. Reel: "What you can see around Puerto Plata's historic center." Visuals: verified walking shots, plaza, cathedral, Pink Street, Umbrella Street.
3. Short: "Three things to check before walking off your cruise ship." Return time, weather/comfort, correct port.
4. Photo post: "A local look at Independence Square." One verified fact and your own image.
5. Short: "Pink Street vs. Umbrella Street: they're different spots." Show actual footage and orientation without revealing all paid guide steps.
6. Post: "Self-guided versus organized excursion: which is right for you?" Neutral comparison; guided excursions may suit travelers preferring transfers or assistance.

## Opt-in messaging flow for a post on your own Instagram profile

Trigger (on an eligible post/reel): commenter types "WALK" to request a free preview.
One initial private reply: "Thanks for your interest in Puerto Plata! Here's the free guide preview: [PUBLIC_PREVIEW_URL]. This route is designed for Taino Bay visitors. Feel free to reply with your port-day question."
No repeated unsolicited follow-ups. Under current Manychat/Instagram rules, a private reply does not itself automatically create a full conversation opt-in; follow the platform's interaction windows and feature requirements.

If user voluntarily replies "Is it safe?":
"Like any city, it depends on the route, the time, weather, and your own comfort. Our route is intended for daytime self-guided visits, but we don't guarantee safety or access on any given day. Keep valuables secure, confirm your ship's departure requirements, and choose an organized excursion if independent walking isn't right for you."

If user asks "Does this start at Amber Cove?":
"No — the current guide begins at Taino Bay. Amber Cove is a different cruise port, and you should not use the Taino Bay walking directions to navigate from Amber Cove."

If user asks "Can I buy in advance?":
"Yes, once checkout is live. We'll explain exactly when and how you receive your PDF and how long the secure download link stays active. Please save the finished PDF for offline viewing when instructed."

If user asks "Can you guarantee return to the ship?":
"No. Ship schedules can change. You are responsible for choosing stops and returning before your own ship's all-aboard deadline, allowing generous time to get back to the port."
